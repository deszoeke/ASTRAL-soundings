# **Arabian Sea pre-monsoon structure** features warm pool warming, wind shear, and jet transitions.

Before the onset of the South Asian summer monsoon in May, the Arabian Sea undergoes dramatic thermodynamic and dynamical transformations characterized by sea surface warming, building moist static energy, and developing low-level jet streams [[1.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/sensitivity-of-the-2023-asian-summer-monsoon-water-vapor-sun-subramanian/a2ce8cb7cd515b2ab42c6dfa842c4d63/) [[2.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-west-asian-surface-pressure-in-summer-monsoon-onset-chakraborty-agrawal/43e1baf704e6528e87e1454eb80509ff/). Understanding these atmospheric and oceanic features requires examining vertical thermodynamic profiles, the evolution of regional wind systems, and observational field campaigns [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/response-of-arabian-sea-to-the-local-forcing-during-2003-chowdary-gnanaseelan/6d133f9c303657f499d02701039a4663/) [[4.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/observed-variability-in-the-thermal-response-of-the-upper-rao/cf30297d080f5d199192d359e1961450/).

## Vertical Thermodynamic Structure

During May, the southeastern Arabian Sea experiences intense solar insolation and weak wind mixing, forming the **Arabian Sea mini warm pool** (ASMWP) where sea surface temperatures regularly exceed 30°C to 30.5°C [[1.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/sensitivity-of-the-2023-asian-summer-monsoon-water-vapor-sun-subramanian/a2ce8cb7cd515b2ab42c6dfa842c4d63/) [[3.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/response-of-arabian-sea-to-the-local-forcing-during-2003-chowdary-gnanaseelan/6d133f9c303657f499d02701039a4663/) [[5.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/arabian-sea-mini-warm-pool-and-the-monsoon-onset-vortex-vinayachandran-shankar/25addaa0b0b75179a585ad384ee89d3b/). Thin, low-salinity surface layers driven by advected Bay of Bengal waters support barrier layers and shallow mixed layer depths, preventing convective overturn and accelerating upper-ocean heating [[6.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-impact-of-barrier-layers-on-arabian-sea-surface-echols-riser/3a54a8e36d8c5e1582058ca480c555f9/) [[6.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-impact-of-barrier-layers-on-arabian-sea-surface-echols-riser/3a54a8e36d8c5e1582058ca480c555f9/) [[7.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/characteristics-of-arabian-sea-mini-warm-pool-during-may-madhusoodanan-rao/696e9333dc935b7cbf6f9797d3b16aae/). This ocean surface warming and associated drop in mean sea level pressure generate localized buoyancy increases below 850 hPa, while boundary-layer moistening raises equivalent potential temperature and moist static energy (MSE) [[1.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/sensitivity-of-the-2023-asian-summer-monsoon-water-vapor-sun-subramanian/a2ce8cb7cd515b2ab42c6dfa842c4d63/) [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/sensitivity-of-the-2023-asian-summer-monsoon-water-vapor-sun-subramanian/a2ce8cb7cd515b2ab42c6dfa842c4d63/) [[8.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-arabian-sea-warm-pool-and-atmospheric-instability-jose-jayachandran/f5b520fde36d509c8e4a4b101dc22a4e/).

* High **sea surface temperatures** exceeding 30.5°C to 31°C peak approximately one to two weeks before monsoon onset [[3.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/response-of-arabian-sea-to-the-local-forcing-during-2003-chowdary-gnanaseelan/6d133f9c303657f499d02701039a4663/) [[7.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/characteristics-of-arabian-sea-mini-warm-pool-during-may-madhusoodanan-rao/696e9333dc935b7cbf6f9797d3b16aae/) [[9.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/characteristics-of-arabian-sea-mini-warm-pool-and-indian-neema-hareeshkumar/47e943b49b4250ffabeee95add64a51e/).
* Moisture increases significantly enhance lower-tropospheric **buoyancy and instability**, elevating equivalent potential temperature between 800 and 600 hPa [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/sensitivity-of-the-2023-asian-summer-monsoon-water-vapor-sun-subramanian/a2ce8cb7cd515b2ab42c6dfa842c4d63/) [[10.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/changes-in-the-thermodynamical-profiles-of-the-subsurface-abhilash-martin/1db7a2d8ca765c62b5fad0a50559623a/) [[8.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-arabian-sea-warm-pool-and-atmospheric-instability-jose-jayachandran/f5b520fde36d509c8e4a4b101dc22a4e/).
* Subsidence from the Arabian heat low and advection of warm, dry continental air establish capping stability and low-level **temperature inversions**, which delay widespread deep convection until cross-equatorial moisture advection peaks [[6.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-impact-of-barrier-layers-on-arabian-sea-surface-echols-riser/3a54a8e36d8c5e1582058ca480c555f9/) [[2.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-west-asian-surface-pressure-in-summer-monsoon-onset-chakraborty-agrawal/43e1baf704e6528e87e1454eb80509ff/) [[2.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-west-asian-surface-pressure-in-summer-monsoon-onset-chakraborty-agrawal/43e1baf704e6528e87e1454eb80509ff/).

## Dynamical Wind Structure

The pre-monsoon circulation is marked by the spin-up of cross-equatorial flow along the East African coast into the Somali or Findlater jet, transitioning the Arabian Sea from weak anticyclonic flow to an intense low-level westerly jet [[11.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/findlater-jet-induced-summer-monsoon-memory-in-the-arabian-kushwaha-kumar/1de212e8167c5482be131bb58e25cfa2/) [[11.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/findlater-jet-induced-summer-monsoon-memory-in-the-arabian-kushwaha-kumar/1de212e8167c5482be131bb58e25cfa2/) [[12.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/contrasting-premonsoon-features-associated-with-early-pattanaik-jayanthi/bb5a8a1086ad52359821984896c366d4/). In early onset years, low-level 850 hPa winds develop during early May and are up to 40% stronger and shifted 2.5° to 3° northward relative to late-onset regimes [[12.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/contrasting-premonsoon-features-associated-with-early-pattanaik-jayanthi/bb5a8a1086ad52359821984896c366d4/) [[2.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-west-asian-surface-pressure-in-summer-monsoon-onset-chakraborty-agrawal/43e1baf704e6528e87e1454eb80509ff/) [[2.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-west-asian-surface-pressure-in-summer-monsoon-onset-chakraborty-agrawal/43e1baf704e6528e87e1454eb80509ff/). Aloft, the subtropical westerly jet retreats north of the Tibetan Plateau, allowing the South Asian High to strengthen and propagate westward, establishing the upper-tropospheric Tropical Easterly Jet (TEJ) around 150–200 hPa [[12.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/contrasting-premonsoon-features-associated-with-early-pattanaik-jayanthi/bb5a8a1086ad52359821984896c366d4/) [[13.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/association-between-regional-summer-monsoon-onset-in-hu-duan/284c29c38b7d5633bcc9bf7a85df0df7/) [[13.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/association-between-regional-summer-monsoon-onset-in-hu-duan/284c29c38b7d5633bcc9bf7a85df0df7/). 

Strong vertical wind shear between low-level westerlies and upper-level easterlies organizes convective complexes [[8.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-arabian-sea-warm-pool-and-atmospheric-instability-jose-jayachandran/f5b520fde36d509c8e4a4b101dc22a4e/) [[8.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-arabian-sea-warm-pool-and-atmospheric-instability-jose-jayachandran/f5b520fde36d509c8e4a4b101dc22a4e/). Concurrently, enhanced horizontal shear of the low-level jet fosters the development of the cyclonic **monsoon onset vortex** (MOV) [[2.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-west-asian-surface-pressure-in-summer-monsoon-onset-chakraborty-agrawal/43e1baf704e6528e87e1454eb80509ff/) [[2.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-west-asian-surface-pressure-in-summer-monsoon-onset-chakraborty-agrawal/43e1baf704e6528e87e1454eb80509ff/). These vortices exhibit potential vorticity maxima in both the lower troposphere (700–850 hPa) and mid-troposphere (~550 hPa), driven by latent heat release and diabatic heating from moist convection [[14.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-structure-of-a-developing-arabian-sea-monsoon-onset-dhavale-aiyyer/c9a2af6fb8625dafbcbac5f9a607cc45/) [[14.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-structure-of-a-developing-arabian-sea-monsoon-onset-dhavale-aiyyer/c9a2af6fb8625dafbcbac5f9a607cc45/). The formation of the vortex draws immense latent energy from the warm pool and accelerates the onset of precipitation along the southwestern coast of India [[15.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/how-the-vortex-over-the-arabian-sea-warm-pool-triggers-an-zhao-sprintall/80c6e68cba055dc9b32832f0be154baa/) [[3.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/response-of-arabian-sea-to-the-local-forcing-during-2003-chowdary-gnanaseelan/6d133f9c303657f499d02701039a4663/).

Field campaigns including ISMEX-73, MONSOON-77, MONEX-79, and the Arabian Sea Monsoon Experiment (ARMEX-2003) provided pioneering shipboard and dropsonde measurements of upper-ocean warming, barrier layers, and onset vortex dynamics [[16.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/on-the-observed-intradiurnal-variability-in-the-thermal-rao-kumar/2f6ad790e2d954598bfdc49d529f598e/) [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/response-of-arabian-sea-to-the-local-forcing-during-2003-chowdary-gnanaseelan/6d133f9c303657f499d02701039a4663/) [[4.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/observed-variability-in-the-thermal-response-of-the-upper-rao/cf30297d080f5d199192d359e1961450/) [[7.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/characteristics-of-arabian-sea-mini-warm-pool-during-may-madhusoodanan-rao/696e9333dc935b7cbf6f9797d3b16aae/). However, direct in-situ boundary layer measurements remain sparse in space and time, leading modern research to rely heavily on reanalysis platforms like ERA5 to diagnose moisture convergence and potential vorticity budgets [[16.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/on-the-observed-intradiurnal-variability-in-the-thermal-rao-kumar/2f6ad790e2d954598bfdc49d529f598e/) [[8.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/role-of-arabian-sea-warm-pool-and-atmospheric-instability-jose-jayachandran/f5b520fde36d509c8e4a4b101dc22a4e/) [[14.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-structure-of-a-developing-arabian-sea-monsoon-onset-dhavale-aiyyer/c9a2af6fb8625dafbcbac5f9a607cc45/). A significant remaining research gap is resolving the exact partition between pure barotropic/hydrodynamic instability and convective diabatic heating during the rapid genesis of the onset vortex across coupled ocean-atmosphere models [[14.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-structure-of-a-developing-arabian-sea-monsoon-onset-dhavale-aiyyer/c9a2af6fb8625dafbcbac5f9a607cc45/) [[14.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-structure-of-a-developing-arabian-sea-monsoon-onset-dhavale-aiyyer/c9a2af6fb8625dafbcbac5f9a607cc45/) [[17.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/balancing-role-of-the-arabian-sea-mini-warm-pool-in-the-lahiri-pant/4fde76f1803c581c974c8300068d790b/).

In summary, the May Arabian Sea atmospheric state is governed by extreme sea surface warming within the mini warm pool, boundary-layer moist static energy buildup, and a rapid transition to strong vertical shear that culminates in the onset vortex and summer monsoon initiation.
